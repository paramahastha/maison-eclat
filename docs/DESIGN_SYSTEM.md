# Design system and art direction

## Subject inventory
Éclat means radiance; lingering presence; collection 01; three interpretations; smoky Obsidian/airy Neroli/warm Santal; top/heart/base; thick glass; brushed cap; ink/brass/ivory; a six-chapter study; the gesture of turning a bottle; a final trace of light.

## Idea and argument
On this page, Éclat's three interpretations of lingering light become three exhibits in one moving study. The visitor changes the composition's light, liquid and notes by choosing a fragrance. This is a fictional editorial concept, not a real perfumery's provenance.

Encounter: a single bottle establishes the subject and collection action. Origin: a glass study shows the original silhouette. Composition: notes expose top/heart/base. Collection: three choices change both visual identity and content. Ritual: the original macro artwork explores light left behind. Invitation: the selected bottle returns with a direct product link.

## Direction
Design-taste `dark-room`, selected for lit objects and exhibit-like negative space. The master plan explicitly requires an oversized left-set headline, warm Origin and six specific chapter interactions, so those override the dealt two-photo hero, unrelated desert imagery and pinned portrait templates. No third-party product photography is claimed. Original procedural art replaces stock landscape photography; no fabricated client proof.

## Tokens and typography
Ink #11120f; ink-soft #25261f; ivory #f3efe6; paper #e7dfd2; brass #b79b68; bronze #7a5d3a; focus #e0b974. Cormorant Garamond 400/italic for display; Manrope 400/500/600 for interface. Fonts bundled locally under SIL OFL. Body 16–18 px. Small captions at least 13 px. Fluid headings and equal outer gutters. Thin underlined actions; square surfaces. Numbering and eyebrows intentionally follow the supplied master plan.

Distinct compositions: split product hero, ivory editorial study, notes orbit and text, horizontal collection selector, full-bleed macro editorial, oversized final wordmark. Mobile has its own stacked product framing. No text may collide with the 3D silhouette.

## Motion and states
GSAP owns intro, reveals and normalized chapter state. CSS handles simple hover/focus. No input scroll interception or mandatory pinning. Reduce motion stops decorative WebGL, removes reveals and preserves every content/selection control. Focus is high contrast; menu uses a modal dialog, focus restoration and scroll lock. Static SVG posters match the original 3D silhouette. Reference screenshots and QA results are in `docs/screenshots` and `docs/QA_REPORT.md`.
